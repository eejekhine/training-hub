PROGRAMME
Rebuilt around your Evri night shift. Shorter sessions, no heavy spinal loading before work, mobility baked into every session to keep your back and hips healthy across a physical shift.
⚠️ PRE-SHIFT RULES
Push and Pull are safe before shift — upper body work won't compromise 8 hours of warehouse lifting.

Legs day only on days off. Heavy squats, deadlifts, and plyos before a manual shift is how you get injured. Don't negotiate on this.
🔵 EVERY SESSION ENDS WITH A SHIFT PREP BLOCK
The last block of every workout is a 8–10 min mobility sequence targeting your lower back, hips, and thoracic spine — the three areas that take the most load during warehouse work. Do not skip it.

PROGRAMME NOTES
🏭
Why this is different to the original programme
The original had heavy trap bar deadlifts, barbell squats, and depth jumps across every session. That's fine when you're recovering at home. Going into 8 hours of heavy warehouse lifting after those movements is a back injury waiting to happen. This version keeps the same philosophy — Beloti strength, ZeroBounce vert — but removes any exercise that compromises your shift or your recovery from it.
🦴
What the shift does to your body
8 hours of heavy manual handling repeatedly loads your lumbar spine, compresses your hip flexors, and tightens your thoracic spine from carrying and bending. The shift prep block in every session directly counteracts this — cat-cow, hip flexor stretch, thoracic rotation, and child's pose reset the areas that accumulate the most load during warehouse work.
⚡
Intensity before shift — stay at 7/10
Don't go to failure on everything before an 8-hour physical shift. Leave 1–2 reps in the tank on every set. Your performance at work matters — going in wrecked every night will catch up with you. Save your absolute max effort for the leg day on your day off.
🍗
Eating around shift + training
Eat a proper meal 60–90 min before training — carbs and protein. Something like rice and chicken, or eggs on toast. Don't train on empty before a physical shift. During the shift, snack on something with protein and carbs on your break. Recover with a meal after shift before you sleep.
😴
Sleep and recovery
Night shift disrupts your recovery rhythm. Prioritise sleep quality over quantity — dark room, cool temperature. On shift days, your body is doing double duty: training recovery and work recovery simultaneously. This is why the sessions are shorter and the intensity is managed — it's not soft, it's smart.
🏀
Court day on Sunday
Sunday is your recovery day and court day. Light shooting, pickup games, skills work. Keep it fun — don't treat it as a conditioning session. It's active recovery that keeps your basketball feel sharp while your body recovers from the week.
