PROGRAMME NOTES
🔄
Swapping with your athlete programme
This is Upper / Lower / Full Body. Your hooper programme is Push / Pull / Legs / Full Body. The muscle groups don't clash. You can run both in the same week — just don't do hooper Legs and this Lower Body back to back. Give a rest day between them.
⚖️
Different weights, same exercises
Same movement, different plates. She picks a weight where the last 2 reps are genuinely hard but form stays clean. You go heavier. The HER and HIM notes tell you when the exercise itself changes rather than just the weight.
📈
Progressive overload
Every 1–2 weeks, try to add a small amount of weight or an extra rep. Even 1kg more on a hip thrust week to week is real progress. Both of you track what you lift so you can see it moving over time.
😴
Rest between sets
No supersets, so take proper rest. 60 sec on lighter isolation work (curls, laterals). 90 sec on compound lifts (bench, squats, RDL, hip thrust). Use the rest to spot each other or talk through form.
🍗
Protein for both
Aim for roughly 1.6–2g protein per kg bodyweight daily. For her: chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese are the easiest sources. For you: same foods, higher total amount. Neither of you needs powder if you're hitting food sources consistently.
🤝
Training together
Spot each other on bench and squats. Use the same machine or station — adjust the weight between sets. Compete on effort and consistency, not on who lifts more. Her progress looks different to yours but it's equally real.
